# User guide prompt for AI
Write a short user guide for our app, for non-technical users. Here's our PRD and screenshots of each screen: [paste or attach].
Give each role its own section: what the app is for, getting in, the two or three main tasks as numbered steps, the rules, and how to get help.
Use the exact words on the buttons and screens. Keep each section to one screen. Don't describe any features that aren't in the screenshots.
