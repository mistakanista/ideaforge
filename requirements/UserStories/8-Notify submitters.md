# Notify submitters

As an employee of Tallis & Reeve, I want to be notified when my ideas are commented on or changed the status, so that I can respond and improve them

## Acceptance criteria
- [+] An email or other notification is sent, when an ideas has been commented or the status changed

- [x] Step 1: the app can send emails (Edge Function, Gmail); only test addresses really receive them, all others are simulated
- [x] Step 2: the submitter gets an email when a reviewer moves or declines the idea
- [-] Step 3: the submitter gets an email when the idea is commented (not started)

## Status: open
