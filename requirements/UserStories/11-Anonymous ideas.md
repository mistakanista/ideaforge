# Create an anonymous idea submission feature

As an admin or employee of Tallis & Reeve, I want to be able to submit ideas anonymously, so that I can share my thoughts without revealing my identity

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Users can submit ideas without providing their name or other identifying information
- [ ] Submitted ideas are clearly marked as anonymous 
- [ ] the name of the submitter is not displayed
- [ ] users still see there own anonymous ideas in their dashboard, but they are marked as anonymous
