# Relate ideas

As an reviewer of Tallis & Reeve, I would like to identify similar ideas and relate or merge them, where the owners remain visible

## Acceptance criteria
- [] a reviewer and only a reviewer can relate ideas
- [] the relation can be duplicates, replaces or is related to
- [] when selecting "duplicates", the other idea gets the relation "is duplicated by" and vice versa
- [] when selecting "replaces", the other idea gets the relation "is replaced by" and vice versa
- [] when selecting "is related to", the other idea gets also the relation "is related to"
- [] confidential ideas cannot be related
- [] a reviewer can also relate his own ideas
- [] there is a button on the detail page, which opens a drop down where the relation can be chosen
- [] in a searchable dropdown all the active ideas are displayed alphabetically for the relation
- [] with a relate button the relation between the ideas is set in the database
- [] the related ideas are displayed on the detail page with the number of votes, the owner and the status
- [] the related ideas are visible for everyone
